## Install

- **Windows**: download `cordfast-v…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and run it (no administrator prompt).
  Or extract the `.zip` into a folder of yours and open `cordfast.exe`.
- **Linux**: extract the `.tar.gz` and run `cordfast` (needs `libxkbcommon-x11`, `libasound2` and OpenGL).

Copies installed this way update themselves to later signed versions.

## What's new

- **Voice: people hear you.** The microphone never reached the call: songbird needs symphonia's PCM decoder for it, and it was off.
- Another of your clients no longer disconnects CordFast when it mutes or leaves, and CordFast no longer hangs on "Connecting" while another client is in a call elsewhere.
- People in a voice channel are listed in Discord's order.
- **Settings in Discord's new layout**: a sidebar with search and your profile card, then Account, Data & Privacy, Messaging Permissions, Notifications, Billing (read-only), Voice & Video, Appearance, Accessibility, System, Language & Time, Registered Games, Activity Privacy and Connected Apps. Privacy, spam filter, friend requests, notifications, activity sharing, embeds, reactions, spoilers, time format and Developer Mode are saved to your Discord account.
- **Discord's expression picker**: GIFs, stickers and emoji in one popout, with Favorites and Frequently Used from your account, each server's icon in the category rail, the skin tone picker and the emoji you point at named under the list.
- **Appearance**: a theme editor that saves your colours as a new theme; each Discord sound can be switched off; the composer's emoji, GIF and sticker buttons can be hidden.
- **Plugins** look like Vencord's, with its required plugins first; every other plugin now starts off.
- Texts, icons and sounds checked against Discord's own client, with Discord's translations in every language; push-to-talk plays Discord's sounds.
- Voice: echo cancellation, automatic gain control and advanced voice activity; the noise suppression popout in the call panel; leaving the call when another device takes it.
- Server tags in the member and voice lists, with the server's card.
- Discord's media viewer, upload cards (spoiler, modify, remove), loading screen, Lottie stickers and embeds in Discord's order.
- Slash commands: typing "/" lists the apps' commands and Discord's built-in ones, with choices and the apps' suggestions.
- Voice channels show what people are playing and listening to, their status, "Set a channel status" and "Invite to Voice".
- The interface speaks eight languages: English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian and Japanese.
- Voice sounds right again, for people and bots alike, and 7.1 headsets get left and right on the front channels only.
- The channel list's width drags at its edge; file dialogs open in the last folder used.
