## Install

- **Windows**: download `cordfast-v…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and open it: it installs at once, with no questions and no administrator prompt, adds the Start menu and desktop shortcuts and opens CordFast at login, as Discord's installer does.
  Or extract the `.zip` into a folder of yours and open `cordfast.exe`.
- **Linux**: extract the `.tar.gz` and run `cordfast` (needs `libxkbcommon-x11`, `libasound2` and OpenGL).

Copies installed this way update themselves to later signed versions.

## What's new

- **0.3.6**: after an update installed when CordFast opens, the window keeps its size and place (it opened at the smallest size).
- **Game overlay**, like Discord's new one: a window over the game, never inside it (no anti-cheat risk). Voice, quick actions, notifications, text chat and friends; Shift+` unlocks it. Settings → Game Overlay. Games must run windowed or borderless.
- **Updates as Discord does them**: CordFast checks for updates when it opens ("Checking for updates…") and installs one before the app starts.
- **Server settings**: emoji and stickers, webhooks, AutoMod, access, pruning and channel permissions.
- **Your account** in the app: username, email, password, authenticator and closing the account.
- **Chat**: search filters, forum posts, the thread browser, bookmarks and reminders, who reacted, polls, big uploads, and videos that play inside their message.
- **Servers**: verification, onboarding, events, discovery and Stage channels.
- **Soundboard** in the call panel; animated emoji, GIFs, stickers and avatars move.
- Settings in Discord's window layout with its Account, Voice and Server Boost pages; noise suppression follows your voice.
